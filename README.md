# Customer_Behavior_Analysis
**📌 Project Overview**
This project focuses on analyzing customer shopping behavior to understand purchasing patterns, spending habits, product preferences, and factors influencing customer decisions.
The objective is to extract meaningful insights that can help businesses improve sales strategies, customer retention, and overall performance.

**❓ Problem Statement**
Businesses often struggle to understand customer purchasing patterns, spending habits, and product preferences. Without proper analysis, it becomes difficult to identify valuable customer segments, evaluate discount strategies, and improve customer retention.
This project aims to analyze customer shopping data to identify trends, understand customer behavior, and provide actionable insights that support better business decisions.

**🎯 Business Objectives**
* Understand customer purchasing and spending patterns.
* Analyze sales performance across product categories.
* Examine the impact of discounts and subscriptions on customer behavior.
* Explore customer preferences based on age and gender.
* Identify trends to support better business decisions.

**🛠️ Tools & Technologies**
* Python: Data cleaning, preprocessing, and exploratory data analysis.
* Pandas & NumPy: Data manipulation and analysis.
* PostgreSQL: SQL queries and business analysis.
* Power BI: Interactive dashboards and data visualization.

**🔍 Project Workflow**
1. Data Cleaning & Preprocessing (Python)
* Handled missing values and checked for duplicate records.
* Standardized column names.
* Created new columns through feature engineering.

2. Data Analysis (PostgreSQL)
* Analyzed customer spending patterns.
* Examined revenue by age group and gender.
* Evaluated discount usage and subscription behavior.
* Identified top-performing products and categories.

3. Dashboard & Visualization (Power BI)
* Created an interactive dashboard.
* Presented key metrics and business insights.
* Used charts and filters to explore customer behavior.

**📊 Key Areas of Analysis**
* Customer purchasing and spending patterns.
* Revenue contribution by age group and gender.
* Discount usage and customer spending.
* Subscription and repeat-purchase behavior.
* Product category performance and customer ratings.

**💡 Business Impact**
* Helps businesses better understand customer behavior.
* Supports data-driven marketing and sales strategies.
* Provides insights to improve customer engagement and retention.

**📁 Project Structure**
* customer_shopping_behavior.csv – Original dataset.
* Python/ – Data cleaning and exploratory analysis notebook.
* SQL/ – PostgreSQL queries for business analysis.
* Power BI/ – Interactive dashboard file.
